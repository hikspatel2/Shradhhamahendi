My Story

A Passion That Became a Profession

✦ ✦ ✦

My journey with mehndi began as a child, watching my grandmother apply beautiful henna patterns during festivals. What started as a childhood fascination evolved into a deep artistic calling. By age 16, I was the go-to mehndi artist for every neighbourhood wedding and celebration.

After completing my formal training in multiple mehndi styles and nail art techniques, I founded Shraddha Mehndi & Nail Artist in 2019 — with a vision to bring world-class artistry to every bride and beauty lover.

Today, with 500+ happy clients and 100+ bridal assignments, I continue to pour my heart into every stroke of henna and every nail design, creating memories that brides and clients cherish forever.
