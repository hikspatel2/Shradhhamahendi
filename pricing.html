FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

✦
How far in advance should I book bridal mehndi?

We recommend booking bridal mehndi at least 2–4 weeks in advance, especially during the wedding season (November–February). For peak dates, booking 2–3 months ahead is ideal to secure your preferred slot.

Do you provide home service?

Yes! We offer home service across the city with a small convenience fee of ₹200. Just mention your location while booking and we'll confirm availability.

Is your henna safe for sensitive skin?

Absolutely. We use 100% natural, chemical-free henna made from pure henna leaves. No black henna, no PPD, no harmful additives — completely safe for all skin types including sensitive skin.

How long does mehndi color last?

With proper aftercare (keeping it on for 6–8 hours, using sugar-lemon solution), mehndi typically stays for 1–3 weeks depending on your skin type, lifestyle, and care. Bridal mehndi with rich natural henna tends to stain darker and last longer.

How long do nail extensions last?

Nail extensions typically last 3–4 weeks with proper care. We recommend an infill appointment every 2–3 weeks to maintain the look and keep your natural nails healthy.

Can I customize my design?

Absolutely! Every design is customized to your preferences. Share your references, outfit details, and ideas — we'll create a unique design just for you. For bridal clients, we offer a free pre-wedding design consultation.
